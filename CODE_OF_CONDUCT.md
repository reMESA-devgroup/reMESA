Code of Conduct

reMESA is an open-source project. Anyone is welcome to participate, contribute, ask questions or criticize our work.

Community rules

- Respect other users and contributors.
- Keep criticism focused on the code and the project.
- Provide evidence when making serious accusations.
- Do not harass, threaten or personally attack anyone.
- Do not share someone's private information without permission.
- Avoid deliberately provoking arguments or disrupting discussions.

Criticism

You don't have to agree with our decisions. Bugs, mistakes and questionable implementations should be pointed out. We are here to improve the driver, not to pretend everything is perfect.

Moderation

Maintainers may remove comments, lock discussions or restrict users who repeatedly violate these rules.

The same rules apply to everyone, including maintainers and collaborators.

One last thing

We like making jokes. That doesn't mean we don't take development seriously.

Have fun, respect others, and enjoy the project!
